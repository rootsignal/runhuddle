# RunHuddle 0.23.0 — more room for your sessions

Replace the navigation sidebar with compact Sessions / Saved work tabs and one-click status filters with counts. Separate Tool and searchable Project controls show the current selections and offer a quick reset. Settings and Help are available in the toolbar; the icon guide also has a Help menu command.

Remove the redundant terminal dropdown. Open terminals through their sessions; starting or unmatched terminals remain accessible in the session list. The resizable list/detail split now uses the full workspace width.

Add **Archive and delete…** with a single review for closed sessions. Codex histories are permanently deleted after archive and scope revalidation; Claude and Copilot histories move to Trash. Running or uncertain sessions are skipped. No history is removed without confirming the review.

Build, 75 Swift tests, 15 cleanup tests, and wide/minimum-width navigation previews verified. This local update preserves running persistent agents.
