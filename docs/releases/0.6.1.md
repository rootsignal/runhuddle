RunHuddle 0.6.1 clarifies the handoff experience.

- Sessions without an explicitly generated AI handoff now show an empty state. Legacy templates are neither displayed as handoffs nor passed to the first AI generation. Any saved legacy text is retained separately as a backup when a generated handoff replaces it.
- Generation shows a readable stage message and explains that it can take a minute or two, especially for longer sessions. You can keep browsing while it runs.
- Existing AI handoffs and your edits remain intact.
