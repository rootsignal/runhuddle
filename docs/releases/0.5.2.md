RunHuddle 0.5.2 fixes sidebar navigation hit areas.

Click anywhere within a sidebar filter row—including its count, padding, or empty space—to switch views. This applies to Now, Pinned, All sessions, and every status filter.

Background activity checks no longer flash the refresh button or footer. Manual refresh still shows progress, including when joining a check already in progress. Checks wait 5 seconds during work/input requests, 15 seconds when idle, and 30 seconds when no real sessions are live. Unchanged session lists are no longer republished or rewritten to the session cache.
